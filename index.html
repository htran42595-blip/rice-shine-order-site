How to order

No account, no card, no deposit. It takes about a minute.

  1. Pick your trays and drinks

    Add as many as you like, then set the mayo, spicy mayo, sriracha, unagi sauce, chive, cucumber and nori on each tray — and the ice on each drink — separately. All free except extra cucumber and extra nori, $1 each.

  2. Choose a pickup day and time

    Fully booked days can't be picked. Times are only the ones inside that day's window.

  3. Leave your name, email and phone

    The email is how your confirmation and any updates reach you, so it's worth double-checking. Instagram is recommended too, in case we need to reach out.

  4. Submit

    Your confirmation email arrives within a few seconds. That's it — you're booked.

You don't wait for approval

Your order is confirmed the moment you place it. We only email you again if something unusual comes up.

Paying for your order

Nothing is charged when you order. You pay when you collect.

Include your Order Reference Code
